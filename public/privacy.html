Privacy

Odds Gods and the Odds Gods ESPN Connector extension. Last updated 20 August 2026.

The short version

The extension exists to do one thing: read your existing ESPN session cookie from your own browser and hand it to the Odds Gods tab you already have open, so we can load your ESPN league. It is not an advertising or analytics tool, and it never sees an ESPN or Disney password.

What the extension reads

  • Two cookies from fantasy.espn.com: espn_s2 and SWID.

ESPN marks espn_s2 HttpOnly, which means no web page can read it. That single restriction is the entire reason this extension exists. It reads nothing else from ESPN, and it reads nothing at all from any other site.

What it does with them

It returns them to the Odds Gods tab that asked. The extension itself stores nothing, keeps no history, and transmits nothing to us or to any third party. It has no server of its own.

Odds Gods then sends those two values to its own server so it can request your league from ESPN on your behalf. They are stored encrypted, keyed to that league, so the league keeps syncing without asking you again.

When it runs

The content script runs only on Odds Gods pages. It does not run on ESPN, and it does not run anywhere else on the web. It responds only to messages from an Odds Gods page and only when that page asks.

What we never collect

  • Your ESPN or Disney password. Sign-in happens on ESPN's own pages.
  • Browsing history, page contents, or cookies from any other site.
  • Anything for advertising, profiling, or sale. We do not sell data.

Removing it

Uninstalling the extension immediately ends all access. To delete the stored ESPN session on our side, remove that league from your account in Odds Gods, which deletes the credentials held for it.
