Checking your ESPN sign in...

  1. 1 Sign in to ESPN On ESPN's own site, in this browser. Your password goes to ESPN and never touches Odds Gods. Sign in to ESPN
  2. 2 Copy your league link and paste it into Odds Gods Open your league on ESPN. Copy the whole web address out of the address bar and paste it into the box on Odds Gods. It looks like this: fantasy.espn.com/football/league?leagueId=1234567 Any page inside your league works. We only need the number. Open Odds Gods

That is the whole setup, once, ever. After this your league re-prices on its own, including on your phone.
