  1. 1 Pin it, so you can see it working Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, find Odds Gods ESPN Connector, and click the pin. The icon tells you at a glance whether ESPN is signed in.
  2. 2 Sign in to ESPN In this browser, in any tab. You sign in on ESPN's own page. Your password never touches Odds Gods, and the connector never sees it.
  3. 3 Come back and press Connect Odds Gods notices the connector on its own, no reload. Paste your league URL and press Connect. That is the last step.
